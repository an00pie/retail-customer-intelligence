Behavioral Analytics

Understand Every Customer

Raw transactions tell you what was bought; RFM analysis reveals customer intent, commitment, and long-term value. We compress complex purchase histories into three behavioral dimensions.

R — RECENCY
How recently did the customer purchase?
Measures days elapsed since last checkout. High recency signals high active interest and campaign receptiveness.
F — FREQUENCY
How often does the customer purchase?
Total discrete orders over lifetime. Measures habituation, brand affinity, and recurring demand velocity.
M — MONETARY
How much does the customer spend?
Gross historical spend volume. Direct indicator of purchasing power, wallet share, and financial impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical Deep Dive & Answers

Everything you need to know about our distributed big data architecture, machine learning models, and real-time serving store.

What problem does the Customer Intelligence Platform solve?
▾
Retail businesses struggle with massive unstructured transactional logs where identifying top customers, predicting future spend, and preventing customer churn is slow and fragmented. This platform provides an end-to-end Big Data pipeline from web mining to HDFS, Hive, Spark ML, and HBase sub-millisecond serving for automated decision-making.
What is RFM analysis and how is it calculated?
▾
RFM analyzes customer behavior along three dimensions: Recency (days since last purchase), Frequency (total number of discrete orders), and Monetary (total lifetime spend). These features are normalized using log1p transformations and standard scaling to power unsupervised K-Means clustering.
Why is HDFS used instead of local file storage?
▾
Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS) provides fault-tolerant, horizontally scalable distributed storage across clusters with 3x replication. It serves as our immutable raw data landing zone capable of scaling to hundreds of millions of transaction rows without single-point failure bottlenecks.
Why is Apache Hive chosen for the data warehouse layer?
▾
Hive provides a schema-on-read relational abstraction over HDFS with columnar Snappy Parquet storage. It cleans 1.06M raw records into 805,549 verified transactions, removes invoice cancellations, drops null customer identifiers, and computes baseline aggregates with high compression efficiency.
Why is Apache Spark used for distributed machine learning?
▾
Apache Spark provides an in-memory resilient distributed dataset (RDD) engine and PySpark MLlib that processes iterative feature aggregations and matrix operations in RAM, delivering ultra-fast feature extraction and model scoring at scale.
Why is Apache HBase used for low-latency serving?
▾
Analytical systems like Hive are optimized for batch scans, not real-time lookups. Apache HBase provides a column-family NoSQL Key-Value store on top of HDFS designed specifically for sub-millisecond point queries (p99 < 1ms) by customer row key.
How does customer segmentation with K-Means work?
▾
Unsupervised K-Means clustering (k=4) groups 5,878 customers into distinct cohorts: Champions (high spend & frequency), Loyal Customers (steady orders), At-Risk (high past spend but inactive), and Lost / Churned (dormant single purchases). Segment mappings are verified in models/segment_mapping.json.
How does CLV prediction work?
▾
Using a 90-day future observation window cutoff, supervised Gradient Boosting regression trains on historical RFM and order tenure features to predict future gross customer value. The model achieves a robust cohort R² of 0.5238 with a p99 winsorization cap at $6,245.56.
How does churn prediction work?
▾
A supervised Random Forest classifier predicts the binary likelihood of customer defection in the upcoming period based on behavioral lapse vectors. It achieves an ROC-AUC of 0.8056 and 75.09% overall accuracy on held-out test data.
